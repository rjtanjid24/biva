About

From microphone to pen, and from pen to clay

I am Saima Biva, from Sirajganj. I am studying Ceramics and Sculpture in the Faculty of Fine Arts at Rajshahi University. My degree is in art, but professionally I found my place as a voice artist.

I regularly voice news content for Update TV. I have also worked on caller tunes and IVR for corporate clients, and on TVC and OVC commercials. Standard Bangla pronunciation and clear delivery are the foundation of my work.

Alongside my voice work, I write. My features and columns on current topics have appeared in outlets such as Jago News, Prothom Alo and Ittefaq. I also enjoy organisational work, and I am involved with the Bangladesh Tarun Column Lekhok Forum (Bangladesh Young Columnists Forum).

With the same hands I shape visible forms from clay, and sometimes paint invisible pictures with words.
